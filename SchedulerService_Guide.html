"Sir/Ma'am, in a hospital, specialized resources like surgeons and operation theaters are strictly finite, exactly like CPU cores. Treatments are processes characterized by an Arrival Time ($AT$), Burst Time ($BT$), and Triage Priority. SchedulerService.java is our core operating system engine that schedules treatments and objectively measures average Waiting Time ($WT$) and Turnaround Time ($TAT$)."
📐 Universal Scheduling Mathematical Formulas:
1. Completion Time ($CT$): Timestamp when the process completely finishes execution.
2. Turnaround Time ($TAT$): Total duration from arrival to completion: TAT = CT - AT
3. Waiting Time ($WT$): Pure idle duration spent in ready queue: WT = TAT - BT
4. Average Metrics: Avg WT = Σ(WT) / N,   Avg TAT = Σ(TAT) / N
